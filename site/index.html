Installing for the first time

Windows and Mac show a warning for apps from small independent developers. It's normal, takes about 20 seconds, and only happens once.

Windows: "Windows protected your PC"
  1. Open the file you downloaded.
  2. Click More info (small link in the blue box).
  3. Click Run anyway. Converto installs and opens.
Mac: "Apple could not verify Converto"
  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Converto into Applications.
  2. Open Converto from Applications. When the warning appears, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to "Converto" was blocked.
  4. Click Open Anyway and enter your password. From then on it opens normally.
